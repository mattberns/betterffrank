how recommendations work

Each available player is scored by the VORP he adds to your starting lineup (1 QB / 2 RB / 2 WR / 1 TE / 1 FLEX) given who you already have. Urgency is what you lose by waiting — his lineup gain now minus the best same-position gain still available at your next turn (opponents drafting by ADP). Once every starter slot is filled it switches to best raw VORP, capped at 2 QB / 2 TE. Check a player off as he is drafted (in order); use undo to correct, off-board for a K/DST or a player not on this board. State is saved locally.
