the board ranks season-long value; this page times positions inside your draft · scarcity, not value

The rankings answer "who is worth the most this season" — and in full PPR that leans WR at the top. VONA (Value Over Next Available) answers a different question: at each pick, how much value do you lose at each position by waiting until your next turn? That is where running-back scarcity actually lives — startable RB dries up faster than WR at the top of a draft, so the timing guide leads RB early even though the value board does not. Assumes opponents draft by ADP; a positional-timing guide, not a full simulator.
